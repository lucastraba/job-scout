# Jane Doe: application packs

This is the example profile, a fictional person. `job-scout guide setup` rewrites it.

## Sources of truth
- `cvs/Jane-Doe-CV.pdf` (in this profile folder) is the **only** source of facts about Jane.

## CV variants
Use the lane's variant unless the posting clearly leans elsewhere:
- `Jane-Doe-CV` leads with the design system and frontend platform work.
- `Jane-Doe-CV-Leadership` leads with team leadership.

## Form answers
- *Why are you looking*: "After building our design system and frontend team from scratch, I'm looking for a role where [the thing this role is about] is the core of the job." Adapt the bracket to the role.
- *Notice period / start date*: "One month's notice."
- *Work authorisation*: "EU citizen, no visa or sponsorship needed in the EU."
- *Location / time zone*: "Lisbon, Portugal (WET). Open to any EU location."
- *Experience*: 10 years building web products; four years leading a frontend team.
- *Salary expectation*: suggest a gross annual figure, marked "(suggested: confirm)". Never below €70k. If the posting lists a range, suggest a figure around its midpoint. Otherwise suggest €75–90k depending on scope.

## Never claim
- That she led more than four engineers.
- Backend or mobile experience.

## Voice
- Concrete and calm; she prefers showing a result to describing a quality.
- Write in the posting's language if it is English or Portuguese.
